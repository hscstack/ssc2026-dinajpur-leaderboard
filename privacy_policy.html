Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SSC Results collects, uses, stores, and protects information displayed on this website.

1. Information We Publish

SSC Results provides publicly available SSC examination results in an organized and searchable format.

The information displayed may include:

  • Student name
  • Roll number
  • Examination marks
  • GPA
  • Result status
  • School/institution name

This information relates to examination results that have already been officially published and are publicly accessible.

2. How We Collect the Data

We do not obtain student results through unauthorized access, hacking, or bypassing any security mechanism.

The results published on this website were provided to us by our representatives across the district after the examination results were publicly released.

Our representatives collected and compiled publicly available result information and provided it to us for organization and presentation on this website.

We do not claim ownership of the underlying examination records. The information originates from publicly released examination results.

3. Why We Publish This Information

We publish the data to make publicly released SSC results easier to:

  • Search
  • View
  • Compare
  • Organize
  • Access by student name, roll number, or school

The purpose is to provide a convenient interface for accessing information that has already been made publicly available.

4. Personal Information

Although some information, such as a student's name and examination result, may be considered personal information, we only display information that was already publicly released as part of the examination results.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information such as:

  • Passwords
  • Private messages
  • Financial information
  • Government-issued identity documents
  • Private contact information

5. Data Accuracy

We attempt to keep the information accurate, but we cannot guarantee that every record is completely free from errors.

If you believe that a result displayed on our website is incorrect, please contact us with the relevant details so that we can investigate and, where appropriate, correct the information.

6. Data Removal and Correction

If you believe that information displayed on this website has been published incorrectly, or that a record should not be displayed, you may contact us and request a review.

We will evaluate such requests based on the source and public availability of the information.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the data stored on our systems from unauthorized access, alteration, or misuse.

However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Third-Party Services

The website may use third-party services for purposes such as hosting, analytics, content delivery, or security.

These services may process limited technical information such as IP addresses, browser information, or request information according to their own privacy policies.

9. Cookies

We may use cookies or similar technologies where necessary for website functionality, analytics, security, or improving the user experience.

10. Children's Privacy

This website does not intentionally collect private information from children. The result information displayed on the website is limited to information that has already been publicly released.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, data practices, or applicable requirements.

Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, believe that information on the website is inaccurate, or wish to request a review of a particular record, please contact us through our designated contact channel.
