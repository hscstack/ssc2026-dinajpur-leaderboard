Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

HSCStack respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use our website and how that information is handled.

Information We Collect

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information.

Depending on how you use the website, we may collect limited technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, and basic usage data for security, analytics, and improving the service.

If you voluntarily contact us, we may receive the information you provide, such as your name and email address.

Result Data

The SSC 2026 result data displayed on this website was collected and processed from publicly accessible examination result information available through the Dinajpur Board's official result website.

The collection process automates publicly available searches and result pages that can also be accessed manually by users. HSCStack does not bypass authentication, access restricted databases, or obtain information that is not publicly accessible.

The displayed student-level result information is limited to educational result fields such as:

  • Student name
  • Roll number
  • School or institution
  • Subject-wise grades or marks
  • GPA and related examination result information

We intentionally exclude information such as students' parents' names and other unnecessary personal details from the dataset displayed on this website.

The underlying examination information is publicly accessible from the relevant examination result system. HSCStack does not claim ownership of the underlying examination data and does not guarantee its accuracy, completeness, or authenticity.

HSCStack is an independent project and is not affiliated with, operated by, endorsed by, or officially associated with the Dinajpur Education Board, the Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education, Dinajpur, or any other Education Board.

Use of Data

The result data displayed on HSCStack is used to provide educational and informational features to students, including result search, school statistics, rankings, and leaderboard functionality.

We do not intentionally sell, rent, or trade personal information to third parties.

Data Removal

If you believe that information displayed on our website should not be publicly available, contains an error, or you would like specific information removed, please contact us. We will review legitimate requests and take appropriate action.

Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services for hosting, analytics, security, content delivery, or other technical purposes. These services may process limited technical information according to their own privacy policies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

For privacy concerns, data removal requests, corrections, or questions regarding this policy, please contact the HSCStack team through our official contact channels.
